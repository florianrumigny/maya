# Visual foundation studies

Use after direction shortlisting when the Foundations branch in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) fires. Compare the unresolved visual choices; retain any approved primitives that still govern the task.

## Derive the system

Translate each shortlisted direction's thesis into one coherent foundation system:

- typography: families, roles, hierarchy, weights, and language coverage;
- color: semantic roles, contrast relationships, and light/dark intent when relevant;
- form: geometry, radii, borders, elevation, density, and spacing rhythm;
- imagery and iconography: subject, framing, treatment, and source strategy;
- material and motion: texture, depth, transitions, and reduced-motion expression;
- signature: one useful, repeatable mechanism identifiable without the logo.

Derive typography and color from the thesis. Test type with realistic language, accents, dates, numbers, short labels, and long content. Test color in functional roles and visible states rather than as swatches alone.

## Compare matched style tiles

Use the same compact content fixture and platform context for every contender. Include enough applied material to expose the system:

- product name and one heading;
- body content and metadata;
- primary action and navigation element;
- image or content object when central to the product;
- active, inactive, and critical state colors.

Show each system as a style tile or small applied composition. Annotate material behavior, motion intent, and its reduced-motion equivalent where a static tile cannot demonstrate them. When available, Taste `brandkit` may create the tile and image generation may render applied UI fragments. A rendered local composition is also suitable. Keep these artifacts in exploration space with their direction IDs.

For each system, name its strongest identity signal, accessibility or localization risk, implementation risk, and likely failure mode. When the tiles determine the shortlist, use [critic-loop.md](critic-loop.md) for their review.

## Foundation gate

Continue when every contender has a matched applied tile covering the six foundation groups above, and the user selects 1-2 systems for screen probes or explicitly delegates that choice.

Hand the tiles and unresolved risks to [mobile.md](mobile.md) for native-mobile screens or [web.md](web.md) for web screens. Keep the systems provisional until they survive a representative screen with real product content; revise a failing foundation before selection. Final formalization follows [design-contract.md](design-contract.md).
